To use this module, you need to:

1. Open a form with a field that has the `confirm_change` attribute.
2. Change the field, then save the record or leave it.
3. In the dialog, click **Save** to save the record, or **Keep editing** to go back to
   the form without saving.

When such a field has changed, switching to another browser tab doesn't save the record,
and closing or reloading the tab asks for confirmation before leaving the page.
